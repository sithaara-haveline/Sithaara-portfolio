I believe the most interesting AI
isn't built from data.
It's built from curiosity.

I'm a second-year student at RSET pursuing B.Tech in AI & Data Science with Honours, alongside a minor in Biotechnology — because I think the future of intelligence lives at that border.

My published research asked: what if AI learned the way infant brains do? Curious, exploratory, not dependent on massive static datasets. That question led to a paper in Conexus, 2025, and it still drives every project I build.

Beyond research, I lead, write, and speak. I'm Secretary of Articon, the departmental AI-DS club. I organise events, coordinate national-level symposiums, and occasionally break boards — 1st Kyu Brown Belt in Karate. Staying in many lanes, on purpose.
